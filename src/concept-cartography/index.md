"Protocol" is used across many fields, and alongside many related notions. This project maps protocol and those notions, with a focus on the distinctions between them, to understand if and when we are talking about the same thing or not.

The cartography is modelled on [Concept Cartography](https://conceptcartography.github.io/conceptcartography/), which grew out of a reading group on epistemic diversity and whose cartographers include two Protocollege members.
