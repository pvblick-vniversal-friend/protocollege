We are writing an essay together, and using it to experiment with the conventions and protocols of academic publishing itself. Four of them are in play:

- **Publishing format.** A target paper with commentaries, rather than one closed text.
- **Authorship.** Publishing the paper as the Protocollege.
- **Crediting.** Digital identifiers that support collective publishing and individual crediting at the same time.
- **Collaborative writing.** How many people write one text together, and the writing structures, governance and digital tools that support it.
