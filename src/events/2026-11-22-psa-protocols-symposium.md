**This session is in person only**, at the 30th Biennial Meeting of the Philosophy of Science Association in San Diego, California. There is no online stream. Check the PSA programme for the room.

Scientific protocols are everywhere in knowledge production and evidence-based policy, from laboratory instructions to disease diagnostics and community engagement. The symposium asks three questions: how do protocols turn abstract norms into specific instructions, what are their virtues and limits as norms caught between stability and variability, and what kind of entities are protocols? The talks connect these questions to reproducibility and epistemic justice.

**Talks**

1. *Developing Protocols for Explanatory Modeling and Measuring: Insights from the Practice of In Vitro Reconstitution*, Andrew Bollhagen (UC Davis)
2. *Protocol Variation and the Methodological Principle of Invariance*, Yoshinari Yoshida (University of Copenhagen)
3. *Creeping Doubt of Chronicity*, Rena Alcalay (TUM)
4. *The Non-Epistemic Dimension of Scientific Protocols*, Daan Kenis (University of Antwerp) and Federica Bocchi (University of Copenhagen)
